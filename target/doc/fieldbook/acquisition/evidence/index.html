fieldbook::acquisition

Module evidence 

Source
Expand description

Durable acquisition evidence bound to the raw-frame journal.

The journal remains the evidence authority. A packet is written to the journal before its versioned evidence extension is persisted. Once the run is closed, the extension is bound to the stable JournalPosition of its journal record and both are verified as one evidence relationship. A mismatch, an absent extension or an ambiguous position fails closed.
