Module capability_registry 

Source
Expand description

Evidence-bound source, product and capability registry with coverage promotion.

Capability claims carry references to retained proof and not the proof bytes. A developer declaration alone cannot raise a capability to Verified, and capability standing is recorded apart from rights standing: a capability may be Verified while rights remain Unknown. Coverage promotion reuses the twelve CoverageState values and admits only explicitly listed steps.

The module declares abstract structures. It names no venue, provider, dataset, rights decision or feed, and it reads no clock.
