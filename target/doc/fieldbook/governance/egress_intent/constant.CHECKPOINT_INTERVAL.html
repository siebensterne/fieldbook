pub const CHECKPOINT_INTERVAL: Duration;
Expand description

Maximum interval between two authorization checkpoints in a receive loop.

A continuously ready socket never postpones a checkpoint: the worker checks its monotonic deadline at the top of every iteration and again after each read, before the frame is processed. This duration also bounds the time a revocation can stay unnoticed when the authority responds promptly.
