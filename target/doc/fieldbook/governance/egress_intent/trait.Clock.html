Expand description

Clock pair used by the authorization lifecycle.

Wall-clock microseconds decide contract validity: a rights record’s effective interval is a calendar fact. Monotonic microseconds decide lease deadlines: moving the wall clock backwards or forwards can never extend or shorten a lease. Keeping the two in one injected trait lets offline tests advance either timeline independently while production uses SystemClock.
